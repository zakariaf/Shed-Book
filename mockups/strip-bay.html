Strip Bay

OFFLINE LAMBING NOTEBOOK · iOS + ANDROID · 390 × 844 · DARK IS THE ONLY BASE THEME · v1.0

A list is a filing decision; a shed is a place, and the shepherd already carries its map in their head. Air traffic control kept paper flight progress strips for forty years after everyone predicted their death, because position carries meaning: each aircraft is a strip in a holder, the arrangement is the mental picture, and controllers never search. Shed Book should be a strip bay — every animal in play is one horizontal strip in a labelled bay, and you never look for her, you look at the wall.

Two inversions make it beat the whiteboard it replaces. First, strips are never wiped, they are FILED: turning out slides a strip off the board into the book, and the board is clean by morning with not one record lost — the one thing paper genuinely cannot do, and the whole argument. Second, nuclear-control-room dark-board discipline: a bay with nothing wrong in it renders as flat black. On a quiet night the board is nearly empty, and that emptiness is the reading — the app telling you, in the strongest visual language available, go back to bed.

Position on the nine design axes

Strip Bay's position on each of the nine design axes
NavigationOne persistent board that morphs, plus a five-key edge rail that re-legends by context. No tabs, no home, no back stack. Secondary functions (medicine book, season, export) push up as a drawer over the board and never replace it. You never go anywhere; the board changes what it is showing you.
DensityDense instrument panel — deliberately the densest of the three. Nine to twelve 72pt strips visible, each carrying tag, bay, hours-since, litter and flag state simultaneously. Every bay caps at five full strips plus a +N MORE strip, so the board never scrolls during an entry.
Typographic voiceHeavy condensed grotesque for identity, tabular monospace for duration. One rule, held everywhere: tags are condensed (a strip has fixed width and the tag must be enormous), every time and elapsed hour is monospaced so a column of timers scans as a shape rather than as prose. Prose reverts to humanist sans.
GeometryHard rectilinear — a rectangle inside a rectangle inside a rectangle: the strip, its holder, its bay. Zero radius anywhere except a 4pt drawer lip. Bay dividers are 2pt rules, not gaps.
Colour strategySemantic multi-hue coding on near-black, confined absolutely to an 8pt strip-holder left edge and never applied to type — the plastic strip boot, from ATC directional coding and raddle-crayon convention. Every coloured edge is redundant with a printed word, so red-shift collapses the hues to a luminance ramp and loses no information. An empty bay has no fill at all: black means fine.
Data representationSpatial map plus bar-and-gauge. Position is the primary datum — which bay a strip sits in tells you its state before you read a character. Each strip carries a horizontal hours-penned fill bar with a tick at the user's own turn-out threshold, so time is a shape readable from three metres, not a number you have to parse.
Primary inputList-tap and two-tap move. Tap the strip, tap the destination bay. Never a drag — ATC's own digital-strip research shows the drag is exactly what fails under load, and gloves make it worse. Arranging bays uses four nudge keys. The keypad is the fallback for an animal not already on the board, never the front door.
Layout anchorRight edge rail, mirrorable — five 72×72pt softkeys down the dominant-hand side, a better one-thumb reach than a bottom bar when the phone is held high over a pen. A tapped strip lifts out of its bay to centre stage at double height with its action row beneath, while the rest of the board holds at 35% so you never lose the shed.
Ornament levelHeavy borders and rules, all of it structural — holders, bays, clip tabs, threshold ticks. No glow, no gradient, no shadow, no texture, no icon, no illustration. Nothing that is not also a boundary.

Both toggles swap real CSS custom properties on the root element. Red-shift is a second full palette, not a filter — all six semantic hues collapse to one ordinal luminance ramp and nothing is lost, because every boot was already redundant with a printed word. Large text runs the whole system at ~160%: the strip goes two-line, the 3-across motor grid drops to 2-across, and the 8pt boot and 6pt hours bar do not scale, because they are instruments, not type.

At arm's length (600 mm) a 44 pt Roboto Condensed cap subtends 29.7 arcminutes — 1.35× the ISO 9241-303 preferred band and 1.85× its minimum. At one metre across a pen it is 17.8′, still above the ISO minimum but below preferred, which is exactly why the hours bar, not the number, is what carries duration at distance. Numerals: tags are proportional (a tag is a name, and the fixed 96 pt cell does the aligning); every time, duration, weight and count is tabular with a slashed zero, because 0/O confusion in a batch number is a safety issue.

Time row 300 × 60 — the two honest states. Safety rule 12.5.

A time the app captured prints the word AUTO. A time a human moved prints the word EDITED and, on its own line and forever, the stamp it superseded — WAS 03:20 AUTO. The original is never overwritten, both stamps stay visible, and there is no third state where you cannot tell which of the two you are looking at. This is the same shape as the superseded slab above (WAS 04:01) and the unchecked-after-checked care box (UNDONE 04:09): in this product a correction is a new row, never an erasure.

No swipe, no drag, no long-press, no pinch, no force touch, no shake, no double-tap. The words Save, Cancel, Submit, Confirm, Are you sure, Loading, Oops, Welcome, Got it, Upgrade and Sync appear nowhere in this product. The rail key that leaves a mode — keypad up, foster in flight, drawer open, strip lifted — is always CLOSE and never CANCEL, because a Cancel implies something was pending, and nothing here is ever pending: the write already happened.

The twelve screens

390 × 844 · NO SERVICE · ONE NIGHT, NOT ONE INSTANT

The twelve screens are one night, not one instant, because the product is used at 4am for entry and at breakfast for read-back. Screens 1–4, 7 and 9 are at 04:00; screens 5–6 at 06:15; screens 8 and 10–12 at 09:40, Monday 3 August 2026. Every number in the dataset resolves against that clock: 412 lambed at 03:20, was penned at 21:00 on Saturday and is therefore 31 h in; the navel dip for her third lamb went overdue 40 minutes ago. The status bar reads No Service on every frame, because the shed has none and never will. Longer screens scroll inside their own frame exactly as they would on glass — the board itself never scrolls, which is enforced by arithmetic in the SHED and PENS morphs.

The 3am test, screen by screen

SPEC §5 · NON-NEGOTIABLE

One thumb, one hand: the rail block is bottom-anchored at y 418–786, entirely inside the right-thumb arc, and NEW exists twice — rail key 1 for muscle memory, the permanent bottom strip for reach — neither of which ever moves. Everything above y 250 is read-only in every state. Gloves and wet hands: the primary target is a 300 × 72 strip, five times the 60 × 60 minimum; the smallest target in the system is the 72 × 72 rail key. No banned gesture: there is no gesture vocabulary at all — one primitive, tap, and nothing else. Moving a strip is tap-the-strip, tap-the-destination; DEL has no hold-to-repeat because a repeat-on-hold is a long-press in disguise; the drawer's lip is decorative and explicitly not a drag handle. Cold fingers: never five across — five 60 pt cells would be nominally legal and practically wrong, which is why birth type and ease are 3 + 2 at 100 × 110, and why there is not one slider, scrubber or thin handle in the product. Darkness: one dark palette and one darker red-shift palette, no light theme to accidentally load, and no splash screen to flash white. Under fifteen seconds: ≈ 3.5 s when she is already lit, ≈ 6.1 s via the keypad, ≈ 8.7 s for a full three-lamb record. Assume the phone dies: every press is a committed write whose timestamp prints on the control, which is why there is no Save button, no draft, no Cancel and no toast — the confirmation is the write, made visible.
