The Register

Shed Book · design system · direction 1 of 3 · amber-phosphor instrument · dark only
You do not navigate to an animal. You load her. Ewe 412 sits in a register at the top of the instrument and stays there — across events, across modes, across a cold launch — until you deliberately clear her. That structurally eliminates the single most dangerous 3am error, recording a lambing against the wrong ewe, because she is always the largest object on the phone. Below the register are twelve keys that never move for the life of the app, and the rule that makes a re-legending instrument safe rather than lethal: a digit always means a number. Press 2 and you have said two; the loaded context decides whether two is lambs, ease or days off, and a welded 26px mode line says which, in words. The keys never change meaning — only what the number is attached to. This is the direction that believes a shepherd on night eleven is an operator, not a user, and that the highest respect you can show an operator is a control layout that never moves. Recall is a readout, never a document: history is a value, not a page.
Position on the nine axes
NavigationNone. One instrument face that re-legends; the loaded subject persists across events, modes and cold launches. No tabs, no back, no stack. Secondary functions are readouts summoned onto the same face by MORE.
DensitySparse and absolutely fixed. Five welded zones, eighteen targets, never more, never fewer, in the same place all night. Density never changes because the layout never changes.
Typographic voiceOversized numeral display over a monospaced instrument face. Tag 120px, key digits 56px, machine text all-caps, tracked, tabular. Lowercase exists only inside a free-text note.
GeometryChunky bevelled slabs on a hard rectilinear grid. 116×76 keys with a 1px top-light bevel and a 2px shadow rule so they read as physical at 10 nits. 10px dead gutters. No radius above 4px anywhere.
Colour strategyAmber-phosphor. One hue at four luminance steps, plus an 800ms commit green and exactly one red used twice in the entire product. Monochrome by construction, so red-shift is the same design dimmed, not a second design.
Data representationNumerals-first readout. The head is an instrument reading; history is a value, not a document — a three-line non-scrolling machine echo of the last writes and nothing more.
Primary inputKeypad-first, exclusively. Tag, litter, ease, dose, days: the same twelve keys in the same positions. Free text is a detour and is announced as one.
Layout anchorBottom thumb-arc. Keys and action slabs own the lower 62% of the display; the register head owns the top and is never a tap target.
Ornament levelMaterial bevel plus confined glow. A 2px phosphor bloom on the register numerals only — functional, because it is how you find the readout in peripheral vision while looking at a lamb. Keys stay crisp so legends never halate.
