# The lambing-ease scale, in long form

Five points, recorded per lambing rather than per lamb. The short labels are the
`vocabEase1`…`vocabEase5` messages; this is the expansion a screen can show
beside them.

**Written for this app.** A five-point assistance scale is common to several
published schemes and the concept is not ownable; the wording below is not taken
from any of them. See `assets/content/vocabulary_provenance.md`.

A blank score is **not a 1**. It means the lambing was not scored, and it is
excluded from both sides of the assisted rate. Nothing infers a score from
silence.

## 1 — No assistance

The ewe lambed unaided. Nobody intervened, including nobody who was watching.

## 2 — Slight assistance by hand

A hand on a leg, a little traction, a lamb repositioned. Brief, and within what
one person does without stopping to think about it.

## 3 — Considerable assistance needed

Sustained effort, or a presentation that had to be corrected before the lamb
could come. Still done in the shed, still by the shepherd.

## 4 — Veterinary assistance needed

Beyond what was manageable without professional help. Recorded as a fact about
what happened, not as a comment on anyone.

## 5 — Caesarean section

Including an elective one. Point 5 covers both, so a planned caesarean has
somewhere to go and does not distort point 4.

---

The scale records **what happened**, and nothing more. It is not a score of the
ewe, of the shepherd, or of the season, and the app never reads it as one: the
assisted rate reports 2-and-above over the lambings that carry a score, states
its coverage, and offers no opinion about the number.
